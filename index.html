clipbot

clipbot is a personal video automation application operated by its owner. It helps the owner prepare and upload short-form video clips to the owner's YouTube channel.

What clipbot does

  • Processes video clips selected by the application owner.
  • Prepares short-form videos for YouTube Shorts.
  • Uploads approved videos to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic statistics such as video view counts for reporting purposes.

Google and YouTube data usage

clipbot uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API only after the Google account owner explicitly authorizes access.

The authorization is used to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and retrieve basic information and statistics about videos managed by the application.

clipbot does not use Google user data for advertising and does not sell Google user data.

Data control

Users can revoke clipbot's Google account access at any time through their Google Account permissions page.
